## HH:MM — Two FIRE calibration fixes: NEI always-iterates + label margin

**Root cause of overconfidence:** model gives high Likert scores regardless of correctness; NEI:5 was paradoxically exiting FIRE; Likert winning score doesn't distinguish "decided" from "torn between two labels."

**Fix 1 — Label margin:** replaced `_winning_score` with `_label_margin` (top score − 2nd score). Margin=4 means model is maximally decided (e.g., Refuted=5, NEE=1). Margin=1 means torn (Refuted=4, NEE=3). `FIRE_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD` now means margin, not raw score.

**Fix 2 — NEI never exits early:** if verdict is "Not Enough Evidence," skip the confidence check and always try another retrieval round. Rationale: NEI literally means "get more evidence."

**Results (--n 5, seed 1):** 3/5 = 60%. Same headline, but Claim 5B now correctly returns Supported ✓ (was NEI ✗ before NEI fix). Post-verdict still fails because 5A is stuck at NEI (Wikipedia 429 + Facebook blocklist = no evidence). FIRE loop now visibly iterates: Claim 3 ran 5 full rounds (margin=3 never exits), Claims 4A/5A/5B hit redundancy stop.

**New issue found:** FIRE_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD=4 (max possible margin) too strict for non-NEI. Claim 3 ran 5 rounds of wasted compute on a correct Refuted verdict. Should lower to 2–3.

**Open issues:** (1) Tune FIRE_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD down to 2 or 3 for non-NEI early exit. (2) Worst-case aggregation penalizes correct sub-verdicts when one sub-claim lacks evidence.
